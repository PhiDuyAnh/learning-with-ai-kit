# Check me

The user decides when to check themselves. Never schedule checks, never nag, never keep a review queue.

Every reply follows **plain-explain**.

## Set up

Ask only what isn't clear:

- **Scope:** a topic, this session, a source, or their notes (for notes, use **notes-check**).
- **Length:** quick (3 questions), normal (5–8), or until it breaks (keep going harder until they miss).

Read `_learner.md` if it exists, and aim at ideas marked shaky or not yet known. Follow the learning profile (see **learning-profile**) for question types and feedback style they prefer or dislike.

## Questions

Mix the kinds that reveal understanding, not just recall:

- **Explain:** "In your own words, why does X happen?" Grade by whether the reasoning is right, not by wording.
- **Predict:** "What happens if we change Y?"
- **Apply:** a small concrete problem or scenario.
- **Spot the error:** a plausible but wrong statement to correct.
- **Multiple choice:** for quick checks. Use the question tool with an "I don't know" option. Write options as bare claims of the same length and shape: write the right answer first, then turn it into each wrong option by applying one real misconception. No reasoning in any option.

Adapt as you go: harder after a right answer, back off after a miss.

## Feedback

After each answer, briefly: right or wrong, the correct answer, and why, in plain words. For a miss, name the likely misunderstanding and fix it with a short explanation. Don't move on until it lands, unless they want to.

## End

Give a short summary: what's solid, what's shaky, and one suggestion for what to look at next. Update `_learner.md`.
